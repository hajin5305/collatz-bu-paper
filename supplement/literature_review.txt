# Independent literature triage for manuscript selection

Date: 2026-10-06. Read-only repository basis: hajin5305/collatz-research, main commit f751b9102355c516434ed9d0882bf4eb8608d95b. This is an intermediate review note for the drafting agent, not a repository modification or a certificate of priority.

## Recommendation

Use RED-BINARY-UNIFORM-PADE-IRRATIONALITY (Theorem BU) as the central result. Its single statement classifies rationality for every binary uniform substitution fixed point and every pair of nonempty output blocks, including nonprimitive substitutions and unequal block lengths and one-counts. This is a broader, more coherent paper topic than the sharp q=481 cycle threshold. Retain the exact classification as the contribution and explain the familiar Padé/height framework openly.

The strongest supported positioning is: a self-contained rationality classification for a specified substitution-generated family, with a moving rational parameter whose height grows subexponentially relative to the substitution scale. No reviewed source has yet been shown to subsume the full statement. This is not evidence of worldwide priority, and the repository's current whole-claim novelty remains unchecked/low.

Do not advertise a first automatic Collatz exclusion, a new general Padé method, a theorem for all automatic or morphic parity words, or transcendence of the entire BU family. The control alphabet has exactly two letters. Variable-length output blocks must remain allowed. The result excludes rational 2-adic realizations of this specified symbolic family; it supplies no theorem placing arbitrary Collatz trajectories in the family.

## Exact statement to preserve

Let sigma be a binary q-uniform substitution, q >= 2, and let t = sigma(t) be a one-sided fixed point. Let U,V be nonempty binary words and kappa(0)=U, kappa(1)=V. If Phi is the inverse shortcut-Collatz parity map, then Phi(kappa(t)) is rational if and only if t is ultimately periodic or UV=VU. In the complementary case Phi(kappa(t)) is an irrational 2-adic integer. Finite prefixes and finite shifts preserve irrationality.

## New primary-source findings

### Brechler: the new multivariate theorem does not automatically close the p-adic bridge

Enzo Brechler, Transcendence of multivariate Mahler functions and algebraic relations between their values, arXiv:2607.24877v1. The manuscript itself is dated February 2026; independent submission-date metadata was not obtained in this agent's successful fetch, so do not conflate manuscript and arXiv dates. URL: https://arxiv.org/html/2607.24877v1

Read Definition 1.1, Theorems 1.2 and 1.6, Proposition 3.12 and its setting, the final proof of Theorem 1.2 in Section 4, and Theorem 5.1. Proposition 3.12 is p-adic analyticity/meromorphy. The value-lifting proof invokes Adamczewski-Faverjon Theorem 3.3 and removes a regular-field-extension hypothesis; it does not state an independently established general 2-adic lifting theorem. Definition 1.1 still imposes a nonsingular transformation, no root-of-unity eigenvalues, a positive Perron-Frobenius eigenvector, contraction to zero, and independence of the orbit. The full BU family has not been embedded in these hypotheses. No whole-paper correctness audit was performed.

### Ide: explicit p-adic multivariate value theorems exist, with material hypotheses

Haruki Ide, Algebraic independence of certain entire functions of two variables generated by linear recurrences, arXiv:1908.06289v1, submitted 17 August 2019. URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.06289 ; theorem text: https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/1908.06289

Read Section 3.1 conditions (I)-(IV)_p, Section 3.2 Lemma 2, and Section 3.3 Theorems 7 and 9. These genuinely include a prime p, so it would be wrong to say that all multivariate Mahler value theory is only complex. Theorem 9 uses a constant-matrix affine subsystem together with separate scalar multiplicative equations, and strong analytic orbit nonvanishing (IV)_p. Lemma 2 verifies nonvanishing under an irreducible characteristic-polynomial hypothesis. The natural binary substitution matrix has eigenvalues q and delta, so this sufficient condition does not directly apply; delta=0 or +/-1 also violates condition (I). No full BU functional-system, nonvanishing, or independence bridge was established. Treat this as relevant prior machinery, not verified subsumption.

## Draft-ready positioning

The inverse of the shortcut Collatz parity map has a classical affine description on finite words. Its arithmetic on a prescribed infinite family is more delicate. Theorem 1 gives an exact rationality criterion for arbitrary nonempty block images of one-sided fixed points of binary uniform substitutions. Neither primitivity nor equal output lengths or equal numbers of ones is assumed. The Thue-Morse specialization overlaps existing p-adic Mahler and Collatz-conjugacy results, and individual automatic itineraries have also been excluded previously. The contribution considered here is the full quantified classification and its self-contained proof.

The argument belongs to the established Padé and arithmetic-height tradition. Its useful feature is a two-variable series evaluated at a sequence of rational points: the main coordinate contracts on the uniform-substitution scale, while the height of an auxiliary coordinate is smaller on that scale. A nonzero approximation of order at least thirteen and degree at most six then yields incompatible 2-adic divisibility and rational-height bounds. General p-adic digit theorems do not apply directly because parity symbols are not the ordinary 2-adic digits of the inverse. Multivariate Mahler value theorems require separate functional-system and orbit-nonvanishing hypotheses; no assertion that all such hypotheses hold for the full family is needed by the proof given here.

## Comparison with the other candidates

- q >= 481: valuable sharp arithmetic classification with a verified finite computation, but tied to a repeated equal-length/equal-sum block partition over a nonconstant least block-list period. No such partition is forced on arbitrary cycles. The sharpness example has minimum 215/481 < 1. The theorem does not prove the same sharp threshold for rational cycles all of whose states are at least one.
- Critical t^(3/5) corridor exclusion: potentially a substantial separate analytic paper, but preserve the width-at-most-one real discrepancy interval, fixed K below the explicit critical constant, and eventual actual orbit hypotheses. This agent did not conduct a fresh theorem-level priority review of that exact analytic statement.
- Length-five all-B cancellation: a clean self-contained classification with precisely two unordered pairs, both at B=15. Its integer-cycle corollary must not be presented as a new small-cycle exclusion. The exact modular classification is the potentially new statement.
- Weak quasipolynomial rigidity (WQ): do not select it on novelty grounds. The latest 2026-10-06 novelty_frontier record classifies it reformulation/high, deriving the full exact conclusion from Monks et al., arXiv:1204.3904v2, Theorem 4.1, p.7, via actual backtracing and lifts.
